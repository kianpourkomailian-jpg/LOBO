The thesis

Why this fits LOBO

Data halls are wall-to-wall repetitive overhead work — cable-tray & containment, busbar, overhead power/data cabling, cooling pipework, CRAC/CRAH & ductwork, fire suppression, lighting. The industry is shifting to overhead cable trays — more sustained work-at-height. And it lands in the exact year an acute M&E labour shortage is pushing rates up, making "fewer man-hours" the #1 contractor buying criterion. LOBO is a painkiller, not a vitamin.
